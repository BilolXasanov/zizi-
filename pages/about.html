01. My Story

Hello! My name is Zizi, and I am a passionate and curious learner with a strong interest in technology, coding, and digital creativity. I have experience working with modern web technologies, including Vue.js, Tailwind CSS, SCSS, and JavaScript, and I enjoy creating responsive, interactive, and visually appealing web projects. Over time, I have developed a keen eye for design, a solid understanding of UI/UX principles, and the ability to turn ideas into fully functional digital solutions. I love working on projects that challenge me to think critically and push my skills further. For example, I have been developing complex Vue.js applications where I structure components thoughtfully, implement modern styling techniques, and focus on scalability and maintainability. I also enjoy experimenting with 3D interactions, animations, and gaming concepts, which allows me to explore creative problem-solving and innovative user experiences.

Beyond coding and design, I am deeply interested in personal growth, self-improvement, and learning new technologies. I approach every project with patience, dedication, and a growth mindset, always aiming to learn something new and improve the final result. I enjoy collaborating with others, sharing knowledge, and exchanging ideas to build better solutions and expand my perspective. In my free time, I like exploring new technologies, working on creative projects, reading, and staying informed about the latest trends in web development and digital innovation. I am motivated by challenges, enjoy solving complex problems, and always strive to combine creativity with functionality in everything I do.

I believe that technology is not just about code—it’s about creating meaningful experiences, connecting people, and making ideas come to life. With a strong foundation in web development, a passion for learning, and a dedication to continuous improvement, I am ready to take on new opportunities, collaborate on exciting projects, and contribute to innovative solutions.
